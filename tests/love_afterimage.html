The Love Afterimage
爱的余像

What kinds of love stay with you — and what kinds do you instinctively leave behind?

Imagine one person you love romantically — or someone with whom you could imagine that kind of intimacy. Twenty small scenes will follow. In each one, choose the detail that catches you FIRST, then the one that matters SECOND. Go with your first reaction rather than what a “good partner” should do.

Two profilesWhat makes you feel loved, and how you tend to love.
Five proportionsNo forced “primary type”; your whole distribution matters.
Hidden dialectsNuances such as quiet presence, reassurance, relief, keepsakes, and comfort touch.

This is a reflective prototype, not a diagnostic or clinically validated psychological test.
